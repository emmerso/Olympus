What We Do

MEAL Consultancy

MEAL — Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning — goes beyond tracking indicators. As a MEAL consultancy, Olympus RETS helps development programmes across Zimbabwe build the accountability mechanisms (community feedback, complaint-response systems) and learning loops that donors and communities both expect.

We work alongside programme teams to design MEAL plans, set up data management systems, train field staff, and facilitate structured learning reviews so that evidence generated during implementation actually changes practice.

This service includes

  • MEAL plan and systems design
  • Community accountability and feedback mechanisms
  • MEAL staff training and mentoring
  • Data quality assurance
  • Learning reviews and after-action reviews
  • MEAL framework alignment with donor requirements
