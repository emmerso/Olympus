What We Do

Training Consultancy

Sustainable results depend on capable teams. Our training consultancy Zimbabwe practice runs practical, hands-on courses in SPSS and NVivo data analysis alongside broader organisational-development topics such as change management, leadership and strategic planning.

Courses are designed around each client's real data and real organisational challenges rather than generic case studies, so participants leave able to apply what they've learned immediately.

This service includes

  • SPSS quantitative data analysis training
  • NVivo qualitative data analysis training
  • Organisational development workshops
  • Change management training
  • Leadership development
  • Strategic planning facilitation
