What We Do

Impact Assessment

Donors, boards and communities increasingly ask not just what was delivered, but what changed. Our impact assessment work measures outcomes and impact against a programme's intended results, using rigorous mixed-methods designs suited to the programme's scale and context.

We have completed impact assessments across sectors including health, water and sanitation, livelihoods, and social protection — work that has directly informed programme design and donor reporting for our clients.

This service includes

  • Outcome and impact measurement design
  • Counterfactual / comparison-group approaches where appropriate
  • Mixed-methods impact studies
  • Sector-specific impact assessments (health, WASH, livelihoods, social protection)
  • Accountability and results reporting for donors and boards
  • Recommendations for scaling or replicating impact
