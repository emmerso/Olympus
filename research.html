What We Do

Research Consultancy

Good decisions start with good evidence. Olympus RETS designs and delivers research studies across Zimbabwe and the wider Southern Africa region — from household baseline surveys to market research and customer satisfaction studies — using methodologies matched to each client's questions and budget.

As a dedicated research consultancy Zimbabwe organisations return to, we combine quantitative surveys with qualitative approaches such as key informant interviews and focus group discussions, giving governments, NGOs, and development partners a rounded, defensible evidence base.

This service includes

  • Baseline and endline surveys
  • Market and consumer research
  • Customer satisfaction studies
  • Demographic and population surveys
  • Needs assessments
  • Quantitative and qualitative data collection design
