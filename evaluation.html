What We Do

Monitoring and Evaluation

Effective monitoring and evaluation Zimbabwe programmes depend on frameworks that are built in from the start, not bolted on at the end. Olympus RETS designs M&E frameworks — indicators, data flows and reporting cycles — that fit a programme's theory of change and donor requirements.

Beyond framework design, we conduct formative evaluations mid-programme to course-correct, and comprehensive end-of-programme evaluations that assess relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, and sustainability against internationally recognised evaluation criteria.

This service includes

  • M&E framework and indicator design
  • Theory of change and logframe development
  • Formative (mid-term) evaluations
  • End-of-programme evaluations
  • M&E system reviews and capacity gap assessments
  • Donor-compliant reporting support
