What We Do

Our Core Service Areas

Three interconnected pillars — designed to take organisations from data to insight, and insight to action.

Research

Baseline surveys, market research, customer satisfaction, demographic surveys and needs assessments using quantitative and qualitative methods.

Evaluation

Design of M&E frameworks, formative evaluation, and comprehensive end-of-programme evaluation services.

Impact Assessment

Comprehensive and incisive impact assessments of projects and programmes to demonstrate accountability and results.

Training

SPSS, NVivo, qualitative analysis, organisational development, change management, leadership and strategic planning training.
