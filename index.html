Who We Are

A World-Class Provider of Business Solutions

Olympus Research, Evaluation and Training Services helps organisations address pressing questions to realise their missions and visions. We serve clients across all sectors — locally and globally.

Based at PSA House, 9 Livingstone Avenue, Harare, our experienced consultant team brings rigour and practical insight to every engagement — from baseline surveys to management training.

Our Core Values

Innovation Continuous Improvement Quality Customer Focus Integrity Diligence

Vision: To be a world class provider of business solutions, through excellent research, evaluation and training services.
Mission: To provide robust research, evaluation and training services to governments, intergovernmental organizations, NGOs and the corporate world.

What We Do

Our Core Service Areas

Three interconnected pillars — designed to take organisations from data to insight, and insight to action.

Research

Baseline surveys, market research, customer satisfaction, demographic surveys and needs assessments using quantitative and qualitative methods.

Evaluation

Design of M&E frameworks, formative evaluation, and comprehensive end-of-programme evaluation services.

Impact Assessment

Comprehensive and incisive impact assessments of projects and programmes to demonstrate accountability and results.

Training

SPSS, NVivo, qualitative analysis, organisational development, change management, leadership and strategic planning training.
